# RUMI Manifesto

**RUMI - Rooted Understanding, Mapping & Insight.**

**Know where everything you know comes from.**

A knowledge keeper that reads what you collect, ties every note to where it came from, connects what belongs together, and answers with the passages behind it.

We save far more than we will ever read. Articles, PDFs, meeting notes, half-finished thoughts. They pile up across folders and apps, copied twice, half out of date, and the one thing you need is never where you look. When you finally find it, you no longer remember where it came from or whether it is still true.

Knowing something means knowing why you know it.

## Root

Every note keeps its roots: where it came from, when it was taken, and which passage it rests on. A summary links back to what it summarizes. A claim without a source is marked as one.

Your notes stay yours. They are plain Markdown in a Git repository you own, readable in any editor. Every change RUMI makes is a commit you can read, question, and undo.

## Understand

What comes in gets read, not just stored. RUMI pulls out what matters, says what kind of thing it is, and notices when new material agrees with, extends, or contradicts what you already have.

Understanding is a proposal until you accept it. RUMI never rewrites your words to sound like its own.

## Map

Notes that belong together get connected. Duplicates are found and, once you opt in, merged with both histories kept. Notes that have gone stale or now disagree with a newer source are flagged, not silently changed.

A pile of notes with no links is still a pile.

## Insight

Ask a question and get an answer with the passages it rests on. Ask what changed, what is out of date, or what to read next, and get a short list with reasons.

When RUMI does not know, it says so and shows what is missing.

## Principles

- **Sources first.** No answer without the notes and passages it comes from.
- **Plain files.** Markdown in Git. No store you cannot read, no format you cannot leave.
- **Every change is a commit.** Reviewable, attributable, and reversible.
- **Propose before you rewrite.** Merges, splits, and corrections start as suggestions until you opt in.
- **Yours to run.** RUMI runs on your own machine. Your notes and credentials stay with you.

## Alongside LINA

RUMI works on its own. Connected to [LINA](https://github.com/thisisjun786/lina), it takes LINA's sense of what matters now as input and reports back what it filed, merged, or flagged, so what you work out in conversation lands where you can find it again. Neither owns the other. You, RUMI, and LINA can all edit your notes, and every change stays in history.

## Family

RUMI is a sibling of [LINA](https://github.com/thisisjun786/lina) and [SION](https://github.com/thisisjun786/sion). LINA is the ally, SION keeps repositories moving, and RUMI keeps what you know.

**A note without a source is a rumor.**
